## #507 definitions

From the brief, read twice: the population is families with **no member aged 65 or older** ("all persons under
age 65") that **had medical expenditures** paid by any source in 2015 (about 97.2 million families; footnote 3:
about 92% of non-elderly families had some). The family follows the CPS definition: relatives by blood, marriage
or adoption in one household. Unmarried partners, foster children and in-laws are separate families, people
living alone are one-person families, and college students count with their parents. OOP is family members'
direct payments, without premiums. The Data Source is the "MEPS 2015 Full Year Consolidated Data File (HC-181)".

Our implementation (the brief names no variables; MEPS's own HC-181 documentation, section 3.5, gives the
recipe):
- **Family:** the CPS family on 12/31/15, key DUID + CPSFAMID. Members are persons with FCRP1231 >= 0 (codebook
  -1 = "not a member of the 12/31 family") and FAMWT15C > 0 ("only persons with positive nonzero family weight
  values are candidates", section 3.5.2).
- **Weight:** FAMWT15C ("poverty-adjusted family weight, CPS family on 12/31/15"), taken from the reference
  person (FCRP1231 = 1), as section 3.5.3 instructs for CPS families. FAMWT15F, the other family weight, is for
  MEPS families, which include unmarried partners, so it does not match the brief's definition.
- **Poverty:** the reference person's POVCAT15 (1 poor/negative and 2 near poor = Poor, below 125% FPL; 3 = Low;
  4 = Middle; 5 = High). MEPS builds POVCAT15 from CPS-family income (documentation 2.5.4.2).
- **Age, adults, children:** AGELAST (age at the end of the year, or when the person was last eligible); adult = 18+,
  child = 17 or younger. Non-elderly means every member is under 65. *The brief does not say how age was
  measured; for 12/31 family members AGELAST equals the end-of-year age.*
- **Family OOP / total:** the sums of TOTSLF15 / TOTEXP15 over members. Families with total = 0 are excluded.
- **Bands:** <$100 means OOP < 100, $100-$2,500 means 100 <= OOP <= 2,500, and >$2,500 means OOP > 2,500.
- **Median, marked underspecified:** the brief does not state the quantile method. Graded value: the SAS PROC
  SURVEYMEANS estimator (linear interpolation on the weighted CDF between neighbouring distinct values; SAS/STAT
  14.1 User's Guide, SURVEYMEANS "Statistical Computations"). The "lower" weighted median (smallest value whose
  weighted CDF reaches 50%) is reported alongside it as an exploratory alternative and is not graded. The 10 median
  cells stay in the denominator, as the pre-registration requires.
- **Diagnostics (not graded):** the weighted number of non-elderly families with expenses (brief: 97,196,954),
  the share with any expense (about 92%), and the poverty-by-structure counts behind Table 1.

**Gate (all must hold, or nothing is graded).** These are the codebook value tables, read 2026-11-04, and the
documentation counts: 35,427 records; FAMWT15C > 0 for 33,850; FCRP1231 = -1 / 0 / 1 for 411 / 20,135 /
14,881; POVCAT15 1-5 = 7,924 / 2,260 / 5,921 / 10,111 / 9,211; TOTSLF15 = 0 for 13,409; TOTEXP15 = 0 for 6,936;
AGELAST between 0 and 85; no negative amounts; exactly one reference person in every family key; and
**14,522 CPS families** with a positive weight (documentation Table 3.3). Each of these tables was checked by
hand to add up to its total.

## #491 definitions

From the brief, read twice: shares (%) of total expenditure for the U.S. civilian noninstitutionalized population.
The Data Source is "MEPS HC-163: 2013 Full Year Consolidated Data File". Hospital services include facility and
separately billed physician payments. Ambulatory care is "office-based settings, hospital outpatient departments,
and emergency rooms".
- **Persons and weight:** PERWT13F > 0 (35,068 of 36,940 in the codebook).
- **Denominator:** TOTEXP13 summed with weights within the group.
- **Type of service:** inpatient = IPTEXP13 (facility + doctor; its facility part IPFEXP13 is labelled "INC 0
  NITES"). Ambulatory = OBVEXP13 + OPTEXP13 + ERTEXP13. Prescribed medicines = RXEXP13. Dental = DVTEXP13.
  Home health and other = HHAEXP13 + HHNEXP13 + VISEXP13 + OTHEXP13. *The brief does not list where vision
  and equipment go; "other medical services and equipment" is our reading.* The job reports how many persons'
  ten components do not add up to TOTEXP13.
- **Age:** AGELAST, <65 vs 65+. In the codebook, AGELAST < 65 (32,823) equals INSURC13 codes 1-3 (32,823).
- **Figure 2 groups:** INSURC13 ("full year insurance coverage status"). 1 = <65 any private, 2 = <65 public only,
  3 = <65 uninsured, 4 = 65+ Medicare only, 5 = 65+ Medicare and private, 6 = 65+ Medicare and other public only.
  These labels match the brief's six groups and its definitions (TRICARE counts as private).
- **Sources of payment (exploratory Figure 3):** Private/TRICARE = TOTPRV13 + TOTTRI13; Medicare = TOTMCR13;
  OOP = TOTSLF13; Medicaid/CHIP = TOTMCD13; Other = TOTVA13 + TOTOFD13 + TOTSTL13 + TOTWCP13 + TOTOPR13 +
  TOTOPU13 + TOTOSR13. The brief's "Other" explicitly includes Medicaid payments for people not enrolled and
  private payments for people without private coverage, which is what the file's "other public" and "other
  private" categories hold. The brief's text gives 14.4% for OOP under 65, while its Figure 3 table gives 14.5;
  the exploratory value is compared with the table.
- **Gate:** 36,940 records; PERWT13F > 0 for 35,068; TOTEXP13 = 0 for 7,831; INSURC13 1-8 = 16,238 / 10,000 /
  6,585 / 1,449 / 1,798 / 769 / 52 / 49; AGELAST 0-85; no negative amounts. These are codebook value tables
  read 2026-11-04, and each was checked by hand to add up to 36,940.

## Matching, files, limits

The matching rules are unchanged: percentages are close within 0.5 points, and medians within 1%. A cell is exact
if our value, rounded half-up to the printed precision, equals the printed number. The raw gap is always reported.
Both files are the fixed-width ASCII zips (HC-163 is offered only as ASCII or SAS transport). They are read with
the codebook inventory's column positions, and each file's SHA-256 is recorded. MV2's hands copy h181dat.zip from
the #506 job folder and download h163dat.zip once in a browser; jobs never download them.

Limits: the median rule and #491's service grouping are our readings. #507's 40 cells measure one population, so
a wrong family definition would move all of them together. The Table 1 diagnostics exist to expose that, but
they are not graded. A failed cell may be our error, not AHRQ's. Standard errors are out of scope.
