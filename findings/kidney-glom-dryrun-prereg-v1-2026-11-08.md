# Pre-registration: kidney pipeline stage 2 dry run. Open HuBMAP glomerulus models on one open KPMP slide (v1)

MV2 of Munim, Inc., 2026-11-08. Written by AI agents (Claude) working in MV2's name; no human has reviewed it yet.
These predictions were fixed before the job runs. Code `kidney/glom.py` 0.1.0 (Apache-2.0), with offline tests in
`kidney/test_glom.py`. Text CC BY 4.0. Research code, not a medical device, not for diagnosis, not medical advice.

## Why

Stage 1 of MV2's one-command kidney whole-slide pipeline (`kidney/wsi_qc.py`, tag v0.9.51) reads the scale, tissue
and focus of each slide. Stage 2 has to find glomeruli, and it should do that without new training. The best open
candidates come from the HuBMAP "Hacking the Kidney" Kaggle competition (2021). Its five winning teams released code
(github.com/cns-iu/ccf-research-kaggle-2021, MIT) and weights (Zenodo 10.5281/zenodo.7730028, CC BY 4.0). We use team
Deepflash2's five kidney U-Nets (`models/5-deepflash/kidney/unet_model-1..5.pth`, efficientnet-b2 encoder). They were
trained on PAS-stained kidney sections at 0.5 um per pixel (Jain et al., Communications Biology 2023,
doi:10.1038/s42003-023-04848-5). The KPMP
slides we use are also PAS, scanned at 0.2527 um per pixel (stage-1 note). Job `hubmap-weights-v2` (2026-11-07)
extracted the 8 Deepflash2 kidney files from the 16.5 GB archive by HTTP range requests (all CRCs correct, about
300 MB fetched).

This dry run asks whether the models load and run on a KPMP slide, and give a plausible count. It is not yet the
comparison with KPMP's own segmentation. That comparison will be pre-registered separately, once we know the format
of KPMP's masks.

## What was seen before this was written (disclosed)

- Stage 1 aggregates for the five slides: 5 of 5 read, MPP 0.2527, a median of 2 tissue pieces per slide, tissue
  covering a median 6.45% of the slide area. No glomerulus has been counted on any slide, by us or by reading KPMP's
  files.
- KPMP documentation: the "Seg_Masks_Features" packages overlay PAS slides with segmentation masks from a model
  trained without KPMP data, with classes including non-globally-sclerotic and globally sclerotic glomeruli.
  We have not opened the masks.
- Deepflash2's published inference code (`models/5-Deepflash2/Inference.py`) uses a zoom of 3 (1536 px read as 512),
  512-px tiles, a step of 0.8 tile, Gaussian blending, the checkpoint's own mean and std, softmax class 1, flip
  test-time augmentation, threshold 0.5, and an empty-tile rule (HSV saturation over 40 on more than 4,000 pixels).
  The copy we read loads colon models, so using zoom 3 with the kidney files is our assumption.

## Method (fixed)

- Slide: `slide02`, the smallest of the five (46 MB). This is the development slide, and it will be left out of the
  later graded comparison.
- Job `kidney-glom-v1` downloads the slide's open package again. It keeps the .svs, writes a local description of
  every other member (format, class names, object counts; never published per slide), and runs
  `python -m kidney.glom` with the five `unet_model-*.pth` files on CPU.
- Scale: the coarsest pyramid level at or below 1.5 um/px is read whole, then shrunk with INTER_AREA to 1.5 um/px.
- Glomeruli: 8-connected regions of the thresholded probability map with area >= 2,000 um^2. Smaller regions are
  counted separately as `small_regions`. The HuBMAP training glomeruli average about 20,125 um^2 (as reported for the
  competition data by Jain et al.).

## Predictions (graded exactly as written)

- **D1 (runs):** all five checkpoints load strictly into `smp.Unet(efficientnet-b2, 2 classes)`, and slide02 ends
  with `ok: true` inside the job's 180-minute limit.
- **D2 (count):** between 5 and 150 glomeruli (inclusive) on slide02.
- **D3 (size):** median glomerulus area between 5,000 and 40,000 um^2.
- **D4 (reference readable):** at least one non-slide member of the package is a mask or an annotation that names, or
  can be tied to, a glomerulus class whose objects can be counted.

If D1 fails, D2 and D3 are not graded, and the failure is reported with its log. Whatever the result, the KPMP
glomerulus count for slide02 is reported only as exploratory.

## Limits

One slide, one scanner, no pathologist reference: D2 and D3 only test plausibility, not accuracy. The zoom setting
is an assumption carried over from the colon copy of the inference code. A 2,000 um^2 floor drops very small
tangential cuts of glomeruli.
