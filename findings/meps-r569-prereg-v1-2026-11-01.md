## Question

Census M1 found that most recent MEPS Household Component Statistical Briefs name only public-use files, so an
outside analyst can at least start a reproduction. R1 is the first actual reproduction: Statistical Brief #569,
"Bone Density Scan (DEXA) Utilization Among Women Without Diagnosed Osteoporosis Aged 50 and Older in the United
States, 2022" (December 2025, AHRQ Pub. No. 25-0093). Its Data Source names one file, HC-243 (2022 Full Year
Consolidated). Its web page (`meps.ahrq.gov/data_files/publications/st569/stat569.shtml`) gives 26 percentages
in 8 figures; we transcribed them twice on 2026-11-01 and both reads agreed (they are fixed in `meps/r569.py`). Two of them (Figure 7, MSA 42.7%, non-MSA 43.3%) need
the MSA indicator, which HC-243 does not carry (see `findings/meps-geo-prereg-v1-2026-11-01.md`). R1 recomputes
the other 24.

## Data

`h243dta.zip` (HC-243, Stata), the same browser download used by check S2 (5,789,896 bytes, 2026-11-01),
copied by MV2's hands into the job folder. The job never downloads it (meps.ahrq.gov/robots.txt disallows
automated fetches of public-use files) and records its SHA-256.

## Definitions (primary, variant A), from the brief's own text and the HC-243 codebook

- Universe: `SAQWT22F > 0` (the brief's sample is women who completed the Preventive Care SAQ, and it says an
  SAQ weight is used for all analyses; SAQWT22F is labelled "FINAL SAQ PERSON WEIGHT, 2022" in the HC-243 codebook),
  `SEX = 2`, age = `AGELAST` (age at end of 2022 or last date of eligibility; if missing, the first
  non-negative of AGE22X, AGE42X, AGE31X), age >= 50. Bands 50-64 and 65+.
- Figure 1 (diagnosed osteoporosis): share `ADOSTP42 = 1` among `ADOSTP42` in (1, 2).
- Figures 2-8 (ever had a bone density scan): share `ADBNDN42 = 1` among `ADOSTP42 = 2` and `ADBNDN42` in (1, 2).
- Figure 3 (ages 50-64): `INSURC22` 1 = any private, 2 = public only. Figure 4 (65+): `INSURC22` 4 = Medicare
  only, 5 = Medicare and private, 6 = Medicare and other public.
- Figure 5: `HAVEUS42` 1 = has a usual source of care, 2 = none. Figure 6: `RACETHX` 1-4 (Hispanic, NH White,
  NH Black, NH Asian). Figure 7: `REGION22` 1-4. Figure 8: `POVCAT22` 1-3 = low (< 200% FPL), 4 = middle,
  5 = high.
- Estimates weighted by `SAQWT22F`; standard errors by Taylor linearisation with `VARSTR` / `VARPSU`
  (reported, not graded).

Sensitivity variants (reported, not graded): B uses the SAQ's own sex item `ADSEX42`; C uses the SAQ age
`ADAGE42`; "A, DEXA universe B" drops the `ADOSTP42 = 2` condition from the scan denominator.

## Matching rule

A cell is **exact** if the recomputed percentage is within 0.05 percentage points of the published one (it
rounds to the published value), **close** if within 0.5 points.

## Predictions (graded by the script and by hand, exactly as written)

- **P1 (primary):** at least 20 of the 24 cells are close.
- **P2:** the headline (Figure 2, women 50+, 42.8%) is exact.
- **P3:** HC-243 contains no MSA variable (none of MSA22, MSA53, MSA42, MSA31, and no variable whose name starts
  with MSA or contains METRO, or whose label has the word MSA), so the two MSA cells cannot be recomputed.
- **P4:** at least 18 of the 24 cells are exact.

If P1 or P2 fails, the cause is diagnosed only in a dated addendum; the definitions above are not changed after
the results are seen.

## Limits

One brief, chosen because it names a single public file that MV2 already holds and because it showed the MSA
gap. A failed cell may be our error, not the brief's. Statistical significance claims in the brief are not
graded. Outputs are aggregate percentages for published cells only; no person-level data leave the job. Any note
to AHRQ waits for a human to check the results.

## Outputs

`r569.json` (file hash, variables found, per-cell estimate, SE, unweighted n, differences, sensitivity variants,
grades), `cells.csv`.
