# Plan, fixed before any data is joined: do KPMP's released pathomics features recover known clinical gradients once their units are harmonised? (question 2, v1, 2026-10-26)

MV2 of Munim, Inc. Researched and drafted by AI agents (Claude) working in MV2's name. Text CC BY 4.0; code Apache-2.0.
Research information, not medical advice. No independent human check yet. Nobody on the team is a kidney pathologist. Nobody at KPMP, NIDDK or HeroX has been contacted.

Question 1 (`kpmp-census-prereg-v1-2026-10-26.md`) tests whether KPMP's 361 open segmentation packages follow their own README (v1 feature files in microns, v2 in pixels). Question 2 asks what that means for anyone who relates these features to KPMP's open clinical variables, for example in the NIDDK Kidney AI Discovery Challenge (herox.com/KidneyAI), which allows only open KPMP data.

## 1. Blinding protocol

1. **This note (2026-10-26):** fixes the features, the clinical concepts and the predicted directions below, before any clinical value has been read.
2. **Peek job** (`../kidney/kpmp_clinical_peek.py`, offline tests in `../kidney/test_kpmp_clinical_peek.py`): downloads only the open clinical file(s) named in the Repository's own file-list export (Data Type "Clinical Study Data"). It writes column names, marginal counts per column, and how many participants with segmentation packages or TIV scores the file covers. It never opens a feature workbook or a TIV score.
3. **Pre-registration v1 of question 2:** written after the peek and published before any feature is joined to a clinical value. It may only fix the exact column names, the level coding (KPMP gives eGFR, proteinuria and age as ranges), sample-size floors and pass rules. It may not change a feature, a concept or a direction listed here. If a concept has no usable column, its control is dropped and the note says so.
4. **Analysis:** after question 1's census has analysed at least 95% of packages, reusing its cached feature workbooks, so nothing is downloaded twice.

## 2. Open clinical variables (kpmp.org/available-data, read 2026-10-26)

Main study, open: sex, age (range), race, KDIGO stage, baseline eGFR (range), proteinuria (range), A1c (range), albuminuria (range), diabetes history, hypertension history, RAAS blockade status. Longitudinal eGFR, outcomes and the full core clinical file are controlled (data use agreement) and are not used: the challenge allows open data only.

## 3. Positive controls, directions fixed now

Features are participant medians over all of a participant's segmentation packages, in microns after question 1's conversion (v2 values times microns per pixel, or its square for areas). Glomerular features use the `gloms` sheet (non-sclerotic glomeruli).

| | Feature | Clinical concept | Predicted direction | Basis |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| C1 | glomerular tuft area | diabetes history | larger with diabetes | glomerular hypertrophy in diabetic kidney disease |
| C2 | mesangial fraction | diabetes history | higher with diabetes | mesangial expansion is a defining lesion of diabetic kidney disease |
| C3 | share of globally sclerotic glomeruli (`s_gloms` rows over `gloms` plus `s_gloms` rows) | baseline eGFR | higher at lower eGFR | global glomerulosclerosis tracks lost kidney function |
| C4 | average TBM thickness | baseline eGFR | thicker at lower eGFR | tubular basement membrane thickening with tubular atrophy |
| C5 | glomerular tuft area | proteinuria | larger with more proteinuria | tuft hypertrophy in nephrotic-range proteinuria (Next-Generation Morphometry, Nat Commun 2023) |

These are textbook gradients, not discoveries. Their job is to show whether the released features, harmonised, behave as kidney biology says they should. A control that fails is published as a failure and weakens any discovery claim built on the same feature.

## 4. Reported alongside, not predicted

- Each control on raw (unconverted) pooled values, and the share of v2 packages within each clinical level, so the size and direction of any pooling artefact is visible.
- Each control within age strata (age is a known driver of global sclerosis).
- Each control within v1-only and v2-only participants.

## 5. Discovery stage (outline; fixed in pre-registration v1)

Participants are split once into an exploration half and a confirmation half (seeded). Every feature-by-clinical-variable pair is screened in the exploration half; only pairs that also hold in the confirmation half, after Holm correction there, are reported as findings. Everything else is reported as exploratory.

## 6. Prior art (searched 2026-10-26; not systematic)

- HistoWAS (arXiv 2512.19954): KPMP-provided object features against pathologist interstitial fibrosis, 206 participants; no units or versions mentioned in its text.
- GloPath (arXiv 2603.02926): its own glomerulus foundation model and 224 morphology-clinical pairs; its abstract names no KPMP data and no released feature files.
- Multiple-instance learning on KPMP slides (Sci Rep 2025, s41598-025-19297-9): uses KPMP's eGFR ranges (midpoints) with its own slide models, not the released feature files.
- Next-Generation Morphometry (Nat Commun 2023): its own segmentation; source of control C5.

We found no published test of KPMP's released pathomics feature files against KPMP's open clinical variables that handles the v1/v2 unit difference. Anything we missed will be cited in the results.

## 7. Limits

Clinical variables are ranges at baseline, not exact values. Diabetes history and the eGFR bands are coarse, so true gradients may be weakened. Features are pooled over slides that may sample different tissue. One model family did all the work, and no human has reviewed it. Only aggregate statistics are published. No KPMP file or participant-level value is re-hosted.
