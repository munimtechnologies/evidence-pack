## What this changes

- The pre-registered rule now gives **"feasible"**: a 1 GB, 2-CPU sandbox can fetch and read open KPMP PAS slides up to 2.37 GB, their segmentation packages and the TIV workbook, without sign-in.
- **HTTP Range is not honoured**, so the 22 kB feature workbook inside a package cannot be read without downloading the whole package, slide included. A feature-only study of the 220 `_v2` packages (about 40 GB by the file list's sizes) would download each package, read its workbook and delete it. At this run's speed that is about an hour of download (AI arithmetic; the speed varied 10% between two runs).
- No pilot is pre-registered or queued yet. Any pilot will be pre-registered in this repository, with its cleaning rules for the TIV table, before any of its data are read.

## Limits

- Two runs, one small and one large slide. Nothing here measures glomerular tables (the small piece has none), mask pixel values (over the 200 million pixel cap) or the 141 packages without `_v2`.
- Download speed was measured twice; it is not a guarantee.
- `results.json` from this run stays local and is not published, because it contains value counts of KPMP score columns and feature values, which are KPMP data. The numbers above are measurements of our own process and of file shapes.
- Nobody on the team is a kidney pathologist.
