## Side finding 1: one DOI, two papers (Crossref metadata)
- `10.1007/s10021-003-0125-0`:
  - Springer's landing page (link.springer.com/article/10.1007/s10021-003-0125-0, read 2026-10-16) shows **Bubier, Bhatia, Moore, Roulet, Lafleur, "Spatial and Temporal Variability in Growing-Season Net Ecosystem Carbon Dioxide Exchange at a Large Peatland in Ontario, Canada", Ecosystems 6:353-367 (2003)**.
  - Crossref's record for the same DOI (api.crossref.org/works/10.1007/s10021-003-0125-0) gives **Zinko, Seibert, Dynesius, Nilsson, "Plant Species Numbers Predicted by a Topography-based Groundwater Flow Index", Ecosystems 8(4):430-441 (2005)**. Other papers cite Zinko 2005 with this DOI.
- The citing paper (PMID 42730795) gives Bubier's title and pages with "8(4)" as volume and issue: those look copied from the Crossref record.
- So a DOI-based check flags a correct citation, and a Crossref lookup turns a correct citation into a wrong one. Not reported to anyone (contact is held for Sheehan).

## Side finding 2: Unicode dashes break publisher-asserted reference DOIs
- 148 "not-found, no-text" references sit in 11 meta-analyses. Nearly all of their deposited DOIs contain U+2010 where the DOI has "-" (e.g. `10.1038/s41572‐020‐00224‐3`).
- The job's cleaner did not normalise dashes, so these never became candidates (they have no title text) and the primary endpoint is not affected. They are still citation links that fail to resolve in Crossref's open metadata.
- **Queued `dash-dois-v1`** (this run) to re-read those 11 reference lists and count how many resolve once the dash is normalised. A quick search found no prior report of this; check again before claiming it.
