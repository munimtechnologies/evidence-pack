# Pre-registration: do the open HuBMAP glomerulus models count KPMP's non-sclerotic glomeruli? (stage 3b, v1)

MV2 of Munim, Inc., 2026-11-09. Written by AI agents (Claude) working in MV2's name; no human has reviewed it yet.
Text CC BY 4.0; code Apache-2.0. Research code and methods, not a medical device, not for diagnosis, not medical
advice.

Written and published **before** any of the 12 slides below has been downloaded or processed by us. Grading code:
`kidney/compare3b.py` 0.1.0 with offline tests (`kidney/test_compare3b.py`, invented numbers only), and
`kidney/kmask.py` 0.2.0, which adds each glomerulus class's areas and changes nothing else.

## Why

Stage 3 (`findings/kidney-glom-compare-results-v1-2026-11-09.md`) compared the released HuBMAP "Hacking the Kidney"
models with both of KPMP's glomerulus classes and failed its count predictions (pooled ratio 0.645). The HuBMAP
labels left out sclerotic glomeruli on purpose (Jain et al., Communications Biology 2023), so the fair target is
KPMP's **non-globally-sclerotic** class. On stage 3's four slides that ratio was 0.838, but this was seen after the
fact and four slides could not separate real agreement from similar counts. Stage 3b tests it on 12 new slides with
stronger controls.

## Slides (fixed now, before any download)

From the list of open KPMP files saved on 2026-10-18 (KPMP's "Segmentation Masks & Pathomics Vectors" packages):

1. keep open packages whose file name ends in `_Seg_Masks_Features_v2.zip` (the v2 format stage 3 read);
2. drop the five packages already used (stage 1-3 slides, including the development slide) and every package from
   those five participants;
3. drop packages over 300 MB (to bound run time);
4. sort by KPMP's internal package id (a random-looking UUID), ascending;
5. keep at most one package per participant (the first in that order);
6. take the first 12.

The list was made by applying these rules to file names, sizes and ids only. Package ids and participant ids stay
in a private input file, per the KPMP data use terms. The slides are called slide3b01 ... slide3b12 locally, in that
order.

## Settings (unchanged from stage 3)

D: `kidney/glom.py` 0.1.1 `glomeruli` (5-model mean probability at 0.5, 8-connected, area >= 2,000 um^2, working
scale 1.5 um per pixel). K: `kidney/kmask.py` as in stage 3's addendum 2, with the same 2,000 um^2 floor.
**K_NS = K - K_GS**, the non-globally-sclerotic objects left after the floor. Nothing is tuned.

## Predictions (graded exactly as written)

A slide is complete when both D and K exist for it. A setup or infrastructure failure is fixed and re-run, not
graded. The predictions are graded only if at least 10 of the 12 slides are complete (the gate); n is the number of
complete slides. All ranges and tolerances are inclusive; ratios are graded unrounded and published to three
decimals.

- **B1 (pooled count):** sum(D) / sum(K_NS) is between 0.80 and 1.25.
- **B2 (per slide):** abs(D - K_NS) <= max(3, 0.25 x K_NS) on at least ceil(2n/3) slides (8 of 12).
- **B3 (control for B2):** B2's number of slides within is at least 2 more than the mean number within over all
  n - 1 non-zero cyclic shifts (D of slide i paired with K_NS of slide i + s). If B3 fails, B2 is reported as
  uninformative whatever its result.
- **B4 (the sclerotic class):** sum(D) is strictly closer to sum(K_NS) than to sum(K). Graded only if the complete
  slides hold at least 5 globally sclerotic objects in all.
- **B5 (rank agreement):** Spearman's rho between D and K_NS across the complete slides (average ranks for ties) is
  at least 0.50. Not graded if either side is constant.
- **B6 (size):** the pooled median area of D's glomeruli divided by the pooled median area of KPMP's non-sclerotic
  objects is between 0.70 and 1.43. Not graded if any complete slide lacks areas on either side.

Also reported, not graded: the share of KPMP's glomerulus objects that are globally sclerotic, and `kmask.py`'s
aggregate line (slides read, pixel size linked, workbook row matches).

## What will be published

Only `compare3b.py`'s aggregate output, the `kmask.py` aggregate line and the run settings. No per-slide count, area,
name, package id or image.

## Limits

Twelve biopsies, one stain. Both sides are models, not pathologists, so agreement is not accuracy. Counts can agree
while objects do not; object-by-object matching needs `glom.py` to export positions and is not part of this test.
The direction of the hypothesis was suggested by stage 3's data (stated above); that is why it is tested on new
slides.
