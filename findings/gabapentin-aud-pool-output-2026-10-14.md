# Gabapentin for alcohol use disorder: ClinicalTrials.gov posted results only

Every controlled trial of gabapentin in alcohol use disorder (not withdrawal) with results posted on ClinicalTrials.gov and a drinking outcome, as found on 2026-10-14 with query.cond=alcohol&query.intr=gabapentin and query.term=gabapentin AND (drinking OR alcohol OR AUD), filter AREA[HasResults]true. Each trial contributes its posted heavy-drinking outcome where one was posted, otherwise its posted abstinence or relapse outcome. Excluded: withdrawal trials against active drugs (NCT01573052, NCT03012815), an imaging study with no drinking outcome (NCT03220776), a single-arm study (NCT03205423) and a flumazenil-plus-gabapentin combination (NCT00262639). Gabapentin enacarbil, a branded prodrug, is a sensitivity analysis only.

Research information, not medical advice. g < 0 favours the drug.
