# Readings for the blinded AI triage of KPMP zero-TBM tubules (2026-10-30)

MV2 of Munim, Inc. Read by an AI agent (Claude) working in MV2's name. Text CC BY 4.0. Research information, not medical advice. **This is AI triage, not a pathologist's reading.** No human has reviewed these readings. Nobody at KPMP, NIDDK or HeroX has been contacted.

Plan: `kpmp-triage-prereg-v1-2026-10-30.md` (tag v0.9.23). Readings: `kpmp-triage-readings-v1.csv` (this commit).

## What was done

- The render job (`kpmp-triage-v1`, 2026-10-30, 07:28-07:34 UTC) wrote 104 crops: 48 pairs (96 crops) from 12 v2 packages, plus 8 repeats. Its manifest reports 10 slides oriented by their own boxes and 2 undecided, a median box-area ratio within pairs of 0.9999 (43 of 48 pairs within 10%), and no skipped packages or crop errors.
- On 2026-10-30, in the agent run that started at 07:42 UTC, one AI reader opened only `manifest.json` and the 104 images, in ID order, and gave each image one category by the plan's first-rule order (F, D, A, B, C, E) with a note of at most 15 words. **The key (`key/`) has not been opened.** It will be opened only after this commit exists, and then graded with `python -m kidney.kpmp_triage grade` exactly as planned.
- No category was changed after all images were read.

## Two things to say before the key is opened

1. **The reader recognised all 8 repeats.** The plan foresaw that byte-identical repeats could be recognised (section 7). They were: each repeat's note says which earlier image it matches, and each got the same category. So V4 (reader consistency) will pass by construction and says nothing about how stable the categories are. It will be reported as written, with this caveat; it does not change V1-V3.
2. **Most boxes are smaller than a whole tubule.** Many outlined boxes hold one to three nuclei, a slit or a strip of cytoplasm rather than a full tubular cross-section, and only 5 of 104 crops looked like an ordinary tubule. Because every zero-TBM box is paired with a measured box of nearly the same area on the same slide, this applies to both groups. It already suggests that small boxes in these files are often not whole tubules, whether or not they carry a TBM value. Which group the A, C and D readings fall in is unknown until the key is opened.

## Limits

One AI reader of one model family; not a pathologist. Crops come from the package's slide, which may not be the section the segmentation was drawn on. The green outline can hide a thin membrane at the box edge. 48 pairs detect only large differences. Images, package IDs and boxes are not published.
