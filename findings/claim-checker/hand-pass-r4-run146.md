## 2. Defects found in R4 as written (each checked against both texts' actual strings)

1. **The operator and the detector don't match (most serious).** The R4 mutation sites in the cut texts are the 5 "as of" anchors (P5 L36, L172; P4 L36, L131, L161) and the 2 elapsed-time durations (P5 L172, P4 L38): 7 sites, fewer than 8, so R4 is already a v4 "shortfall" module scored on what exists (P2 then needs 5 of 7). Deleting the anchor at P5 L36, P4 L36, P4 L131 or P4 L161 leaves a sentence with **no R4 trigger at all**: "not published", "the only registered studies", "exactly three ... exist", "no ... is registered, and none has posted results" are not in R4's trigger list, and the unanchored test exists only for "not yet". **So at most 3 of 7 R4 mutations can be detected under v4 as written, and R4 fails P2 by construction**, whatever the code quality.
2. **"Recompute with every date the package gives" is not implementable.** It never says which date a span starts from or ends at. P5 L172's start is "primary completion estimated 2024-12-31" on L171 (a sibling sub-bullet), with a different event word ("planned completion" vs "primary completion estimated"). P4 L38's start is "first approved 1960-01-21" on L9 (the Overview), with no shared word ("market" vs "approved"). The end is also unspecified: measured to the deadline, P5 L172 is false (2024-12-31 to 2027-03-26 = about 26.8 months, not "nearly 22") although the sentence is correctly anchored ("as of 2026-10-20": 21.7 months, true). The package's own notes say "keep the date anchor" is the intended fix, so measuring an anchored span to the deadline would be a **false flag on a correct line**.
3. **No rule for durations that cannot be recomputed.** 20 of P5's 21 "N months/years" hits are not elapsed-time claims. If code flags what it can't recompute, that is 20 false flags on P5 alone, enough to sink P3 (precision >= 0.50). v4 doesn't say.
4. **A span followed by "after" isn't always elapsed time.** P5 L172 also has "MOAKS cartilage score at 24 months, after ACL tear": a comma-separated "after" that is clinical context, not a date span.
5. **"not yet": boundary, case and quotes unspecified.** Substring matching adds 3 "cannot yet" hits (epistemic statements, no date sense). Case-insensitive matching adds P5 L167, a quoted registry status whose anchor is "(as read 2026-10-16)", which is not the "as of" form, so it would be flagged. P5 L66 "not yet enough to change practice" is an evaluative judgement, not a dated fact, and is flagged under every reading. **Predicted false flags on the unmutated cut texts under v4, depending on those choices: 1 (word-bounded, case-sensitive) to 5 (substring, case-insensitive), plus 0-21 more depending on defect 3. True flags: 0.**
6. **Anchor forms.** The texts anchor dated reads as "as of <date>", "(as read <date>)", "rechecked <date>", "checked <date>", "searched <date>", "unchanged on <date>", "re-read ... on <date>". v4 knows only "as of", and its case is unspecified (P4's two are "As of").
7. **Anchor scope.** P4 L131's second and third sentences ("None from another country or group exists ...") are anchored only by the bullet's bold lead "**Result (as of 2026-10-20):**". P5 L173's "no posted results" and "unreported" are anchored only by the block lead on L163 ("**Registered trials** (ClinicalTrials.gov, searched 2026-10-15 and 2026-10-20 ...):"). Sentence scope alone would flag both (false flags). Section scope would be too wide: in P4 E.5, a deleted "As of 2026-10-20" on L161 would still be "covered" by L158/L159's "rechecked 2026-10-20", hiding the mutation.

## 3. Proposed v5 definition of R4 (not adopted; for the v5 plan file)

- **Text R4 reads:** sentences of the cut text outside the reference list, tables and quotation marks. Emphasis stripped. Word boundaries as in v4's name matching ("-" and "_" are not boundaries, so "pre-registered", "self-reported", "cannot" don't match "registered", "reported", "not").
- **Anchor:** a date (YYYY-MM-DD, YYYY-MM, or "D Month YYYY") directly preceded by "as of", "as read", "rechecked", "checked", "searched", "accessed", "unchanged on", or "re-read ... on" (case-insensitive). **Anchor scope:** the sentence; the bold lead of its own bullet or paragraph; and the governing block lead (the nearest preceding non-bullet bold-lead paragraph ending with ":", for bullets directly under it). Never wider.
- **R4-a elapsed span:** "N days|weeks|months|years" (digits or one..twenty; optional "about", "nearly", "almost", "over", "more than") followed **immediately** (no punctuation) by "after", "since", "ago" or "on the (US )?market". Start date: the one date whose preceding 4 words hold the event word ("completion" -> completion; "market" -> approved|approval|marketed), searched in order in the same sentence, then the same bullet block (parent bullet and its sub-bullets), then the Overview; the first level with exactly one candidate wins, else the span is "unbound" (listed as info, never flagged). End date: an anchor **in the same sentence only**, else the deadline (2027-03-26). Flag when the recomputed length (calendar months + days/30.44) breaks the qualifier: "nearly/almost N" needs N-1 <= x < N; "about N" |x-N| <= max(1, 0.1N); "over/more than N" x > N; bare N |x-N| < 1. Every other duration is not an R4 claim.
- **R4-b status claim needing an anchor:** in one clause, a limiter (no, none, not, not yet, only, exactly <number>, almost no, currently, still) before one of registered | posted | published | recruiting | exist | exists; or the word "unreported". Flag it when no anchor is in scope. ("reported" is left out: in these texts it mostly describes what a fixed paper says, e.g. P5 L200 "no CI reported". Known miss: P5 L18 "the only adequately reported randomized trial".) The v4 stand-alone "not yet" rule is dropped; "not yet" now counts only as an R4-b limiter.
