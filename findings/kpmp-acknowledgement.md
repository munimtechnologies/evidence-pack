Medicine Project (KPMP), and the code in `kidney/` when it is run on KPMP data, is covered by the acknowledgement
below. It is KPMP's own requested text, copied from the "Citing KPMP data" block of KPMP's citing page
(https://www.kpmp.org/help-docs/study-overview?tabname=citingkpmpdata) on 2026-11-11, with "Month Day, Year"
filled in. The access date of a given note is the date its data were downloaded, given in that note.

## Correction (2026-11-11)

Notes published from October to 11 November 2026 carried a shortened paraphrase of this text: the opening
funding disclaimer was missing, the funding sentence was reworded, and the closing sentence was replaced by "We
thank the KPMP participants and US taxpayers whose support made the data possible." The stage 6 pre-registration
used a closer but still incomplete version. That was our error. On 2026-11-11 the acknowledgement section of the
five kidney-glomerulus results notes was replaced with the text above, and nothing else in them changed:

- `findings/kidney-glom-dryrun-results-v1-2026-11-09.md`
- `findings/kidney-glom-compare-results-v1-2026-11-09.md` (stage 3)
- `findings/kidney-glom-stage3b-results-v1-2026-11-10.md`
- `findings/kidney-glom-stage4-results-v1-2026-11-10.md`
- `findings/kidney-glom-stage5-results-v1-2026-11-11.md`

Pre-registrations are left exactly as published, because their text is the dated record of what was planned;
this file is their acknowledgement. The earlier `kpmp-*` notes (October 2026) are also covered by this file and
will be corrected in place when they are next updated. All new notes use the text above.

KPMP's data use terms also apply: notes here publish pooled aggregates and code only, never images, crops,
per-slide or per-participant values, or package ids. KPMP has not reviewed or endorsed any of this work.
